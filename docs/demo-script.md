# Demo script (2 minutes)

1. **Problem (20s):** Blind people bump into head-height obstacles canes miss, and constantly lose track of where they put things.
2. **Obstacles (20s):** Walk toward a judge → beeps speed up.
3. **Memory (40s):** A judge hides your keys while you walk around the room. Say "Hey Guide… where are my keys?" → spoken answer.
4. **Companion app (20s):** Show the live view and memory cards on screen.
5. **How it works (20s):** Two models we trained (wake word on the Arduino, object detector) + Gemini for answers.

Backup: press SPACE if the wake word misfires. Have the recorded video ready.
